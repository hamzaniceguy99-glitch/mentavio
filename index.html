How we work

Coaching, and clear about the line

This is performance coaching. We are not licensed psychologists and this is not therapy, diagnosis or treatment. We work on routines, attention and the habits around competition.

If what's going on is bigger than sport — persistent low mood, anxiety beyond competition, disordered eating, anything involving self-harm — the right support is a licensed professional, and we'll tell you so directly rather than coach around it. In the US, 988 reaches the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Is this sport psychology?

No. Licensed sport psychologists are trained clinicians who can assess and treat. We're performance coaches working on routines and habits. If you need the former, we'll say so.

Which sports do you work with?

Individual and team sports both. The mental skills are largely the same; the routines get built around your sport's specifics.

My child is 13. Can they join?

Yes, enrolled by a parent who can attend sessions. We also have a program for parents, which is often where the fastest change comes from.

I choke in big matches and I'm embarrassed.

It's one of the most common reasons people contact us, and it responds well to structure. It is not a character flaw.

Will this make me win?

No honest coach promises results. We work on what you control — preparation, attention, recovery after mistakes — and those tend to show up in performance.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

What happens on game day?

Twenty minutes on your sport and where competition and practice diverge. Free.
