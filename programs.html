The programs, in detail

Four programs of 6 to 12 weeks, built around your real competitions.

Program 01 · Use the adrenaline

Competition nerves

Nerves aren't the problem — being unprepared for them is. We build a routine you run before every competition, practice breathing that settles the body quickly, and rehearse the opening minutes, since that's where most athletes lose the plot. If anxiety extends well beyond sport, we'll say so and suggest a licensed professional.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any sport
  • 10 min a day
  • Competitions to use

Start this program

Weeks 1–2 · What happens

Mapping your own signs of nerves, physically and mentally.

Weeks 3–4 · The routine

A repeatable sequence before every competition.

Weeks 5–6 · Breathing

Settling quickly, practiced in training first.

Weeks 7–8 · Opening minutes

A plan for the start, rehearsed until it's automatic.

Weeks 1–2 · Where attention goes

Noticing when and where you drift.

Weeks 3–4 · Cues

A word or action that brings you back.

Weeks 5–6 · Process goals

Targets you control, instead of the scoreboard.

Weeks 7–8 · Under fatigue

Holding it when you're tired, which is when it matters.

Program 02 · One point at a time

Routines & focus

Focus isn't concentrating harder; it's having somewhere specific to put your attention and a way back when you drift. We build cue words, set process goals you can control, and practice the reset between points or plays until it's habit.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any sport
  • Fits your training
  • Training sessions

Start this program

Program 03 · After it goes wrong

Confidence & slumps

Confidence follows evidence, and slumps destroy the evidence you can remember. We rebuild deliberately: tracking what's actually going well, changing what you say to yourself after mistakes, and structuring a return that doesn't depend on one good result. For depression, eating concerns or persistent low mood, we refer to a licensed professional.

  • 10 weeks
  • Any sport
  • 15 min a day
  • Willingness to track

Start this program

Weeks 1–3 · The honest picture

What's actually happening versus what it feels like.

Weeks 4–6 · Self-talk

Changing what you say after a mistake, deliberately.

Weeks 7–8 · Evidence

Keeping a record that survives a bad week.

Weeks 9–10 · Forward

A plan that isn't riding on one result.

Weeks 1–2 · Your part

Honest look at what you bring to their competition days.

Weeks 3–4 · After the game

What helps, and the questions to stop asking.

Weeks 5–6 · The long view

Keeping them in sport, which matters more than this season.

Program 04 · The car ride home

Parents of young athletes

Young athletes consistently report the car ride home as the worst part of competition. We work with parents on what to say and not say, on separating your investment from their performance, and on how to talk to coaches. This is coaching for the parent; we don't work directly with children.

  • 6 weeks
  • Parents
  • 1 hour a week
  • A young athlete at home
